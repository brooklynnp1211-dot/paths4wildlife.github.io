FEATURED RESEARCH

Evaluating the Functionality of Expert-Assessed Wildlife Corridors with Genetic Data from Roe Deer

Bukhart et al.

This study examines whether wildlife corridors identified by experts function as intended by using genetic data from roe deer. The research provides an example of how molecular evidence can be used to evaluate wildlife connectivity across fragmented landscapes.

WHY THIS STUDY MATTERS

Connecting genetics to conservation

🧬 Genetic Data

Genetic information can provide evidence about movement and connectivity between wildlife populations.

🌲 Gene Flow

Movement between populations can allow genes to be exchanged, providing evidence of connectivity across a landscape.

🦌 Corridor Function

Comparing genetic patterns with proposed corridors can help researchers evaluate whether those corridors are functioning as intended.
